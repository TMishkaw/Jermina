# Golden Acting Standard for Voice & Performance

## 1. Core Principles of Organic Voice Synthesis
Synthetic voices must never sound robotic, monotone, or artificially generated:
- **No Robotic Laughter:** Never write or synthesize literal phonetic laughs (`«ха-ха-ха»` or `«хе-хе»`). Real human laughter is integrated through words, chuckles, breath drops, and vocalized expressions:
  - `«Пфф!.. Та ні, ви серйозно?..»`
  - `«Ой, не можу... Стривайте...»`
  - `«Хех... ну це вже занадто!»`
- **Dynamic Cadence & Pauses:**
  - Introduce strategic dramatic pauses (`...`) before comedic punchlines or impactful revelations.
  - Vary speaking pace: faster inquisitive delivery during hooks, deliberate grounded pacing during conceptual takeaways.

---

## 2. Pacing Constraint (5–10 Presets per Short)
- **Beat Density:** Vertical videos must feature exactly 5 to 10 deliberate acting beats (`target_count_range=(5, 10)`).
- **Hold Duration:** Each gesture must sustain staging for $\approx 3.5\text{–}5.5$ seconds to allow mobile viewers to absorb the body language.
- **Strict Prohibition:** Spastic cue flickering ($<1.5\text{s}$) and rapid prop flashing are strictly forbidden.
